About ClearPath Debt Guide

Debt is confusing by design. Every company promises the fastest fix, the biggest savings, the easiest way out, and it's genuinely hard to tell which of those claims are real. We built ClearPath Debt Guide to cut through that noise: a straightforward starting point for understanding your options before you commit to any of them.

What We Actually Do

We're an independent resource, not a lender, and not a debt relief company ourselves. Our free assessment asks a few honest questions about your debt, income, and payment history, then points you toward the kind of approach that tends to fit situations like yours: consolidation, settlement, credit counseling, or another path entirely.

From there, we connect you with partner companies who specialize in the option that seems to match your circumstances, so you're not starting your search from scratch or wading through a hundred search results to find someone who actually handles your situation.

How We're Compensated

We're upfront about this because we think you deserve to know it going in: when you're referred to one of our partners and you choose to move forward with them, we may receive compensation. That's how we're able to offer the assessment and our educational content for free.

That compensation doesn't change what it costs you to work with a partner, and it doesn't come at the expense of an honest assessment, our questions are built to point you toward what actually fits your numbers, not toward whichever partner pays the most. You can read the full details in our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

What We Believe

  • Understanding your options should be free, no cost to take the assessment, no obligation to act on it.
  • You deserve a straight answer, including when a program isn't a good fit for your situation, not just when it is.
  • Debt relief isn't one-size-fits-all, which is why we ask real questions instead of pushing a single product.
